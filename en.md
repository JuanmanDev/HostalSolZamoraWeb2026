Phone support: 10:00–21:00. Check-in: 14:00–21:00. The hostel closes at 21:00 with no exceptions — arrivals after 21:00 without prior agreement will not be accepted. Once you've checked in, you'll receive keys to come and go as you please. Most Sundays we're closed, but if you arrive Saturday you can stay through Sunday without issue. We're open most holidays except Aug 14–16, Dec 15 and Jan 1.

[Direct link](https://hostalsolzamora.com/en/faq/what-are-the-hostels-hours) [ Full check-in guide](https://hostalsolzamora.com/en/checkin)

Check-in is from 14:00 to 21:00. If you arrive before 14:00 we can store your luggage at reception. During low occupancy we may offer early access to the room, but it's not guaranteed. Check-out is at 11:00. After check-out we can also store your luggage for the day (except Sundays) — just let us know. If you arrive after 21:00 we cannot receive you under any circumstances.

[Direct link](https://hostalsolzamora.com/en/faq/what-are-the-check-in-and-check-out-times) [ Full check-in guide](https://hostalsolzamora.com/en/checkin)

Under Royal Decree 933/2021, all guests aged 14 and over must present a valid official photo ID: national ID card (DNI), passport, NIE, TIE or EU driving licence. Expired, damaged, illegible documents or those sent by email or WhatsApp are not accepted. Each guest must show their own ID — the booker's ID alone is not enough. To speed up the process, we recommend using Chekin online before arrival: you'll receive a link to photograph your document and submit the required data. If you complete Chekin online, you only need to show at least one registered ID upon arrival. Otherwise, all guests must present their physical document at check-in.

[Direct link](https://hostalsolzamora.com/en/faq/what-id-do-i-need-for-check-in) [ Complete online check-in](https://hostalsolzamora.com/en/checkin) [ Document types accepted](https://hostalsolzamora.com/en/checkin)

The hostel is on the 3rd floor of a building with 2 lifts. There are no steps at the entrance or to access the rooms. You can check photos and videos of the rooms in our gallery to see whether the space and dimensions suit your needs. If you have any questions, contact us and we'll assess your specific case — we can recommend the most suitable room.

[Direct link](https://hostalsolzamora.com/en/faq/is-the-hostel-accessible-for-reduced-mobility) [ View room gallery](https://hostalsolzamora.com/en/galeria)

The hostel has no private parking, but several options are very close. Parking Plaza Constitución (3 min walk, €1.45/h, €14.70/day, open 24h) is the nearest. Parking Plaza de la Marina Española (5 min, book online at Empark) is another option. For free parking, the Train Station (2 km, bus L3) offers 24h free parking. Right outside there's an ORA (blue) zone at €0.35/30min, max 3h. Although it's a pedestrian zone, the City Council allows vehicle access for guests via Calle San Miguel to drop luggage.

[Direct link](https://hostalsolzamora.com/en/faq/where-can-i-park) [ Full parking guide](https://hostalsolzamora.com/en/parking) [ Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir//Calle+Benavente,+2+Zamora)

The train station and bus station are next to each other on Avenida de las Tres Cruces, about 2 km from the hostel. Your options: taxi (5–7 min, approx. €6–8), urban bus Line 3 (El Acercame, €0.95) — get off at the "Mercado" stop, 5 min walk from the hostel — or on foot in about 25–30 min. The train station has free 24h parking and left-luggage facilities.
