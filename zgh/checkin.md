Check-in # Amek Ara Tafeḍ d Amek Ara Txedmeḍ Check-in

Asgid tallit s tallit i wakken ad tafeḍ hostel d ad teɣreḍ izerfan ixuṣṣen s unagraw.

## Deffir Tili

Qqen-d d tzemreḍ ad tewweḍ hostel deffir 20:50. Hostel yettegg deffir akud-nni.

Sexdel-d s Chekin: ad d-ternuḍ ufrar i wakken ad tezgazeḍ deg Chekin, fad ara tserkeḍ tiwlaf n usnilfu-nnik (DNI, Asefsar, ...) d akken ad tefkeḍ akk ixrayen ixuṣṣen. Nxuṣṣ talɣut n akk imuddar ɣef Umarur Aɣelnan 933/2021.

Taɣimt-ik tetthazeg deg 14:00. Ma tusa-d deffir tilia, nzmer ad nesɣel kus-nik ɣer akud-nni.

## Check-in s Usiɣen s Chekin — Itedduu yakan

Ma txedmeḍ check-in s usiɣen deffir tili, uselkim deg talalt itedduu yakan bezzaf. Tanmmirt!

## Deg Talalt Tewweḍ-d

Imellalen ad skednin tenzert-ik. Tzemreḍ ad tfeyḍeḍ ma yella ayen (tigddamin tizdatinin, tigeldiwin n umẓi, wussan nniḍen, ...). Izerfan n akk imuddar ad ttwaskdnent. Ma check-in Chekin s usiɣen tenna, uselkim dɣa itedduu yakan. Ad k-ssiwlen amek ara seqdeḍ tsaratin i us-kkeḍ ɣer hostel s wamek tebɣiḍ. Ma tbɣiḍ asexsi n uqercuc neɣ tasnifest, ɣer talalt s wura. Teɣed 3 tsaratin d ad k-sselken amek ara tent-seqdeḍ i tewwurt n ufrag, n hostel d n taɣimt-ik.

## Deg Taɣimt

Tallalt amek ara qq ɣef WiFi, akud n uffaɣ d isɣalen n hostel: asnfaq yettwaxrez (tiγrim ɣer 1.000€ i usfey d usefsas), tisni tettwaxrezza d yideɣ n yid tettwaxrezz.

## Check-out

Ẓu tsaratin deg tablut i tettaf deg tuzni deffir n liftit. S tfadda, ffeɣ taɣimt deffir 11:00 i team n usafar am wakud. Nzmer d nesɣel kus-nnik deg ass n uffaɣ (ɣar Ahad), mudd-aɣ-d awal d nesserɣ.
