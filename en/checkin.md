Check-in # How to arrive and check in

A step-by-step guide for getting to the hostel and preparing the documents required by law.

## Before you arrive

Make sure you can arrive at the hostel before 20:50. The hostel closes after that time.

Register with Chekin: you will receive a link to register on Chekin, where you can take a photo of your identity document (ID card, passport, etc.) and send all the details we need. We require information for all guests under Royal Decree 933/2021.

Your room will be ready at 14:00. If you arrive earlier, we can store your luggage until then.

## Online check-in with Chekin — faster arrival

Completing online check-in before you arrive makes the reception process much quicker. We really appreciate it!

[Complete online check-in](https://guest.chekin.com/NpW1pWVoQ3CWVMB1augWwA-housing)

## Arriving at the hostel

[Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir//Hostal+Sol,+Calle+Benavente,+2,+3%C2%BA,+49014+Zamora,+Espa%C3%B1a/@41.5062474,-5.7435342,18z/) [ Apple Maps](https://maps.apple/p/5NVDS6BJpi3Ihy) [ Waze](https://www.waze.com/es/live-map/directions/es/cl/zamora/hostal-sol?navigate=yes&to=place.ChIJ72vc5TAeOQ0RRra7dNoyQyc) [ Bing Maps](https://www.bing.com/maps?q=Calle+Benavente+2+49014+Zamora&lvl=17)

1

Go to Calle Benavente 2

You can arrive on foot, by taxi or by car. If you come by car, check the parking options — you can access from Calle San Miguel to drop luggage.

[View parking options →](https://hostalsolzamora.com/en/parking)

2

Enter the building

The door is usually open — push it. If it's locked, ring the third floor on the right-hand panel, or call us directly.

3

Go to the third floor

You can use either of the 2 lifts or take the stairs.

4

Find us

We're right here. Press the doorbell and we'll attend you. If no one appears after two minutes, call our phone number. Note: use standard calls — WhatsApp or VoIP calls are not monitored.

## At reception

Staff will check your reservation. You can pay for any extras (extra beds, cots, additional days, etc.). All guest IDs will be checked — if you completed online check-in via Chekin, this will be much faster. Staff will also explain how to use the keys to access the hostel at any time. If you need a hair dryer or iron, this is the time to request it.

## In your room

You will find instructions on how to connect to WiFi, the departure time, and house rules: smoking is strictly forbidden (fines of up to €1,000 for cleaning may be applied), as is making fire or noise at night.

## Check-out

Leave your keys in the mailbox you will find in the corridor before the lifts. Please leave the room by 11:00 so the cleaning team has enough time to prepare the rooms for the next guests. We can also store your luggage during the day of departure (except Sundays) — contact us and we'll arrange it.

## Required documentation (R.D. 933/2021)

Under Royal Decree 933/2021, we must register the details of all guests aged 14 and over and share this data with the Security Forces and the INE (National Statistics Institute).

Full name

Surnames

Document type (National ID / Passport / NIE / Residence card)

Document number

Support number / serial number (if applicable)

Issue date

Expiry date

Date of birth

Country of birth

Country of residence

Autonomous Community (if Spain)

Nationality

Sex

Contact email

Contact phone number

INE — Tourism statistics: Under the Public Statistical Function Act, the National Statistics Institute (INE) requires the country of origin or autonomous community of each guest to compile national tourism statistics. This data is transmitted anonymously and in aggregate form.

[View the BOE regulation](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2021/11/01/pdfs/BOE-A-2021-17808.pdf)
