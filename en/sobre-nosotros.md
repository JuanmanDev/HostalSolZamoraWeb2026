About us # A welcoming hostel run by people who love what they do

Welcome to our home — a different, modern, fresh place that wants to give you the rest you deserve in a warm and quiet atmosphere. Travellers from all over the world have come through our doors, and each one has left their mark.

Since 2023, Hostal Sol has been run by a family with more than 50 years of experience in hospitality. We've been through change, inexperience and fear. After plenty of effort and work — and a pandemic that brought everything to a halt — we've moved the hostel forward, giving our home everything it deserved and learning from every guest who's stayed with us.

When the rooms were empty, we got to work renovating. We painted and redesigned each room into a Nordic-minimalist style, where rest, closeness and comfort are the core values.

Brand philosophy ## Closeness, calm, family feel

Our philosophy rests on three pillars: closeness, calm and family feel. We carry that philosophy into every detail, from the moment you arrive until you head off again. Our care is personal, respectful of your space and your tastes, and always centred on your rest. Each traveller is on their own path — we want to be your quiet corner in the heart of a beautiful city like Zamora.

A place to feel at home ## Comfortable, affordable, hassle-free

Our hostel is the perfect spot for travellers looking for a comfortable, hassle-free stay. Simple, welcoming rooms with everything you need: quality bed linen, soft towels, private bathroom with shower and free Wi-Fi throughout.

At reception we'll happily help with information about nearby sights, the best restaurants and bars in the area, and any other questions during your stay.

Unbeatable location ## In the heart of Zamora

We're lucky to be in an incredible spot: smack in the centre of Zamora, between the two main streets, and a 3-minute walk from Plaza Mayor and the historic old town. You can enjoy the city's history and culture on foot, without ever needing the car. Bars, restaurants and parking are nearby too — without losing the quiet of a small street.

Over 50 years of history ## Pensión Padornelo · Hostal Sol

Our story begins in 1975, when the family opened Pensión Padornelo on Calle Aire in Zamora. For many years that family guesthouse was a reference point for travellers arriving in the city.

Over time, Hostal Sol was born on Calle Benavente, alongside the Restaurante Padornelo, a tribute to the original name. Over 50 years of hospitality back every stay.
