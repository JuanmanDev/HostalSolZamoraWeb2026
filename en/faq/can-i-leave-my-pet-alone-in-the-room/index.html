FAQ

Can I leave my pet alone in the room?

No, this is not a pet hotel. Animals must always be accompanied by a human at all times. They cannot be left alone in the room, the hostel, the car or anywhere else. If this rule is not respected, the pet cannot be accommodated and the guest will be offered the option to cancel the booking with a penalty for failing to follow house rules.
