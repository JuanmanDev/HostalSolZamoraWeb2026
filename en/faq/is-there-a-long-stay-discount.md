# Is there a long-stay discount?

Yes, from 11 nights onwards you get an automatic 10% discount on the total price. The discount applies to non-refundable rates. If it doesn't work correctly on the website, contact us and we'll apply it manually. We also offer group discounts — if you're travelling with friends or family, ask us.
