# What bed does my room have? Can I switch to a double bed or twin beds?

The bed matches the room type you booked: double rooms have a 135 cm wide bed and twin rooms have two single beds. You'll see it in the room name on your booking, and the gallery has photos and videos of each room. If you'd like to switch from twin beds to a double bed, or the other way round, write to us and we'll try, but it depends on availability and isn't always possible on busy dates. If the bed type matters to you, the safest option is to choose the right room when you book.
