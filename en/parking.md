Parking # How to park near Hostal Sol Zamora

The hostel has no private parking, but we are in the historic centre with several very close options.

**Hostel address**

Calle Benavente, 2 – 3rd floor (2 lifts) · 49014 Zamora

Although it is a pedestrian zone, the Zamora City Council allows vehicle access for hostel guests via Calle San Miguel to drop off luggage.

[ View check-in guide →](https://hostalsolzamora.com/en/checkin)

## Car / motorbike access

You can access the hostel by car or motorbike from Calle San Miguel. Although it is a pedestrian zone, the City Council allows guest access for luggage drop-off. Once unloaded, park in one of the options below.
