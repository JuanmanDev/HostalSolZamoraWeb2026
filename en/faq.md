##

Under Royal Decree 933/2021, all guests aged 14 and over must present a valid official photo ID: national ID card (DNI), passport, NIE, TIE or EU driving licence. Expired, damaged, illegible documents or those sent by email or WhatsApp are not accepted. Each guest must show their own ID — the booker's ID alone is not enough. To speed up the process, we recommend using Chekin online before arrival: you'll receive a link to photograph your document and submit the required data. If you complete Chekin online, you only need to show at least one registered ID upon arrival. Otherwise, all guests must present their physical document at check-in.

##

The hostel has no private parking, but several options are very close. Parking Plaza Constitución (3 min walk, €1.45/h, €14.70/day, open 24h) is the nearest. Parking Plaza de la Marina Española (5 min, book online at Empark) is another option. For free parking, the Train Station (2 km, bus L3) offers 24h free parking. Right outside there's an ORA (blue) zone at €0.35/30min, max 3h. Although it's a pedestrian zone, the City Council allows vehicle access for guests via Calle San Miguel to drop luggage.

The train station and bus station are next to each other on Avenida de las Tres Cruces, about 2 km from the hostel. Your options: taxi (5–7 min, approx. €6–8), urban bus Line 3 (El Acercame, €0.95) — get off at the "Mercado" stop, 5 min walk from the hostel — or on foot in about 25–30 min. ##

IFEZA (Instituto Ferial de Zamora — Zamora's trade fair and events venue) is about 4–5 km from the hostel. You can get there by urban bus Line 5 (El Acercame, €0.95) from stops in the historic centre — check the official route for the nearest stop — by taxi (approx. 10 min, €8–10) or by car. Important tip: during major events and concerts, organisers often run free shuttle buses (lanzaderas) from Zamora city centre to IFEZA and back. Always check the event website or IFEZA's site before travelling so you don't miss the free service. Popular events at IFEZA include: Fromago (International Cheese Fair, every spring), Conciertos Zeta (large-scale rock and pop concerts), SIFER (International Railway Fair, alternate years), food & agriculture fairs, job fairs and many more throughout the year.

Direct link [ IFEZA — Official website](https://www.ifeza.es/) [ Line 5 — El Acercame](https://www.acercame.es/index.php/linea/linea5) [ IFEZA — Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir//IFEZA+Zamora)
