Vía de la Plata # Vía de la Plata pilgrims: your stop in Zamora

Zamora is one of the stages of the Vía de la Plata to Santiago. If after the day's walk you'd rather have a private room with your own bathroom, Hostal Sol is right in the city's historic centre.

From €40 per night

## A private room you can book ahead

No shared dormitory: you get a private room with en-suite bathroom, bed linen and towels, and you can book it in advance on our website with 15% off. Prices from €40 per night.

## Keep your bike in your room

If you ride the Camino, you can keep your bike in your own room as long as it is clean. Let us know when you book and we'll give you a larger room if possible. There are two lifts, and you can also carry it up the stairs to the third floor.

## Arrival from 14:00 to 21:00

Check-in is from 14:00 to 21:00, with no exceptions after that time. If you finish your stage early, we'll keep your backpack. Reception is closed on most Sundays: if you arrive on a Sunday, ask us before booking.

## Walking with your dog

Pets are welcome for €7 per night. They must always be accompanied and cannot be left alone in the room.

## Breakfast

We don't serve breakfast, but there are cafés less than 2 minutes' walk from the hostal.

## Book your night in Zamora

From €40 per night, with 15% off when you book on our website.
