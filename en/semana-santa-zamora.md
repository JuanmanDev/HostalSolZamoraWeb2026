Holy Week # Holy Week in Zamora, from the heart of the old town

Zamora's Holy Week (Semana Santa), declared of International Tourist Interest, is one of the oldest and most moving in Spain. Hostal Sol is right in the historic centre, so you can walk to the procession routes and back to rest without a car.

From €40 per night

## Holy Week 2027 dates

In 2027 Holy Week runs from Palm Sunday, 21 March, to Easter Sunday, 28 March. These are the busiest days of the year in Zamora: if you want to sleep in the centre, book months ahead.

## Night processions, with your own keys

Many processions leave at night or in the early hours. Once you have checked in you get your own keys and can come and go whenever you like, including late at night.

## Arrive before 21:00

Check-in is from 14:00 to 21:00 and cannot be done later, Holy Week included. Plan your trip to arrive before then; if you arrive before 14:00 we'll keep your luggage so you can go out and see the city.

## Coming by car

Many streets in the centre close to traffic during the processions. The hostal has no garage of its own: leave your car in a nearby car park before the closures start and get around on foot. Our parking guide lists the closest options.

## Outside Holy Week: the Holy Week Museum

If you visit Zamora at another time of year, the Museo de Semana Santa displays the floats of Zamora's brotherhoods and is the best way to understand the celebration.
